## Planche de direction artistique

Fichier : `images/proposition-collection-v1.png`.

```text
Use case: ui-mockup / stylized-concept.
Create ONE polished visual direction board showing THREE large collectible cards for EDUCA, an original French children's reading game. Landscape image, all three complete portrait cards shown flat and straight on, equal height, ratio approximately 5:7, generous even gaps, rounded corners, on a quiet deep navy background. No surrounding app interface, hands, desk, device or perspective mockup.
The new collection should feel emotionally collectible, illustrated and premium, while readable and welcoming. Original high quality stylized 3D storybook illustration, rounded sculpted forms, expressive faces, rich material detail, warm cinematic lighting, vivid but controlled color, subtle foil edges, no excessive glare. Each central scene occupies roughly two thirds of its card. Visual hierarchy, broad silhouettes readable at thumbnail size. No existing game characters, no imitation of an existing trading-card brand.
Kaya is the original mascot: a friendly baby JAGUAR, golden fur, distinct dark rosette spots, round ears, big warm expressive eyes and short muzzle. Same visual character design wherever Kaya appears.
Card 1, blue/teal frame, phonics card: one beautiful small MOTORCYCLE, clearly identifiable, parked and unoccupied on a forest path; child friendly rounded design, no rider. The motorcycle is the dominant subject. Show a very large, perfectly readable LOWERCASE "m" on a calm pale header panel, rounded educational sans serif, with clear two humps. Bottom French text exactly "Le son m" and below "comme dans moto". This is the existing m/moto teaching association. A small sound-wave emblem, not combat statistics.
Card 2, violet frame with warm gold details, achievement card: Kaya joyfully opens a large beautiful book; its pages illuminate his face in a miniature lush jungle clearing, magical but gentle. It celebrates a first reading lesson, not combat. Header text exactly "EXPLOIT". Footer text exactly "Première leçon". Small book achievement emblem.
Card 3, warm gold frame with restrained holographic shimmer, chess-progression card: a magnificent recognizable CHESS ROOK, sculpted like a friendly guardian tower with battlements, resting on an elegant little stone chess pedestal with a few jungle leaves. The rook, not a castle landscape, is the unmistakable main subject. Header text exactly "TITRE". Footer text exactly "Tour". Small rook emblem.
Shared restrained cream text panels with dark high contrast lettering, consistent frame construction, crisp typography, artwork remains visible rather than buried in effects. No extra text, tiny unreadable labels, power/health points, random rarity labels, packs, weapons, photorealism, frightening expressions or watermarks. This is a proposal board, designed to make the jump from emoji/SVG illustrations to genuinely illustrated collectible cards immediately obvious.
```

Create a standalone portrait illustration expanding the MOTORCYCLE artwork in the first card of the reference to occupy the entire canvas, approximate aspect ratio 5:7.
